# Sound effects: sources and licences

All 12 files (`claw-click`, `thud`, `whoosh`, `key-tick`, `marker-swipe`, `ding`, `buzzer`, `pop`,
`stamp`, `chime`, `rimshot`, `sting`) are **original, synthesised by `scripts/make_sfx.py`** from
mathematical waveforms and noise. No samples, no third-party material.

- Licence: CC0 (public domain) by the Koding Konkani project. Safe for YouTube and Instagram.
- They are placeholders that sound simple. To upgrade one, drop in a recorded sound with the same
  file name and add a row below: file, source URL, licence, date, and whether attribution is needed.
